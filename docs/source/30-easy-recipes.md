## Paneer tikka sandwich

**Serves: 1 \| Prep: 10 min \| Cook: 8 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   100 g paneer

-   2 slices bread

-   1 tbsp yogurt

-   0.5 tsp tikka masala

-   2 tbsp onion

-   2 tbsp bell pepper

### Method

1\. Mix crumbled paneer, yogurt, spice, onion and pepper.

2\. Microwave in a safe bowl for 2 minutes, stir, then heat in 30-second
bursts until hot; or pan-cook 4--5 minutes.

3\. Fill bread and toast in a pan or sandwich press, if available.

### Cook with me script

1.  Step 1: Mix crumbled paneer, yogurt, spice, onion and pepper.

2.  Step 2: Microwave in a safe bowl for 2 minutes, stir, then heat in
    30-second bursts until hot; or pan-cook 4--5 minutes.

3.  Step 3: Fill bread and toast in a pan or sandwich press, if
    available.

## Shortcut aloo paratha

**Serves: 1 \| Prep: 10 min \| Cook: 12 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   1 medium potato

-   2 pieces tortillas or paratha dough

-   0.25 tsp cumin

-   1 pinch chilli powder

-   0.25 tsp salt

-   2 tsp oil or ghee

### Method

1\. Pierce potato with a fork; microwave 4--6 minutes, turning halfway,
until soft. Cool, peel and mash.

2\. Mix potato with cumin, chilli and salt. Sandwich between tortillas
or fill/seal dough.

3\. Pan-cook with a little oil for 2--3 minutes per side.

### Cook with me script

14. Step 2: Mix potato with cumin, chilli and salt. Sandwich between
    tortillas or fill/seal dough.

## One-pot masala macaroni

**Serves: 1 \| Prep: 5 min \| Cook: 15 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   90 g macaroni

-   0.25 small onion

-   1 small tomato

-   0.25 cup frozen peas

-   0.25 tsp garam masala

-   1 tbsp ketchup

-   2 cups water

### Method

1\. Cook chopped onion in a little oil for 2 minutes; add tomato and
masala for 1 minute.

2\. Add macaroni, peas, ketchup, salt and water; bring to a boil.

3\. Simmer 8--10 minutes, stirring often, until pasta is tender and most
water is absorbed.

### Cook with me script

    and masala for 1 minute.

29. Step 2: Add macaroni, peas, ketchup, salt and water; bring to a
    boil.

## Quick garlic chilli oil

**Serves: 4 \| Prep: 3 min \| Cook: 2 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   0.25 cup neutral oil

-   1 tbsp chilli flakes

-   1 clove garlic

-   1 tsp sesame seeds

### Method

1\. Put chilli flakes, finely minced garlic and sesame in a heatproof
microwave-safe bowl.

2\. Warm oil separately in short 20-second microwave bursts until hot,
not smoking; carefully pour over chilli mixture.

3\. Cool, refrigerate promptly and use within 4 days because it contains
fresh garlic.

## Microwave mug brownie

**Serves: 1 \| Prep: 3 min \| Cook: 1 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   4 tbsp all-purpose flour

-   2 tbsp sugar

-   1 tbsp cocoa powder

-   3 tbsp milk

-   1 tbsp neutral oil or melted butter

-   1 tbsp chocolate chips

### Method

1\. In a large microwave-safe mug, mix flour, sugar, cocoa and a pinch
of salt.

2\. Stir in milk and oil until smooth; add chocolate chips if using.

3\. Microwave 45 seconds, then use 10-second bursts until mostly set.
Rest 1 minute; mug will be hot.

# Occasional meals

## Shortcut pav bhaji

**Serves: 1 \| Prep: 10 min \| Cook: 15 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   1 medium potato

-   1 cup frozen mixed vegetables

-   1 medium tomato

-   0.5 small onion

-   1 tsp pav bhaji masala

-   1 tbsp butter

-   4 pieces pav/bread rolls

### Method

1\. Pierce potato and microwave 4--6 minutes, turning halfway, until
soft. Cool, peel and mash. Microwave vegetables with a splash of water
until soft; mash roughly.

2\. Cook onion in half the butter for 2 minutes; add tomato and pav
bhaji masala for 3 minutes.

3\. Add potato, vegetables, salt and a splash of water; mash and simmer
5 minutes. Toast pav in remaining butter.

-   1 tsp oil

-   1 pinch black pepper

## Easy dal and rice bowl

**Serves: 1 \| Prep: 3 min \| Cook: 5 min \| Difficulty: Easy**

### Ingredients

-   0.75 cup cooked or canned lentils

-   1 cup cooked rice

-   2 tbsp water

-   1 pinch turmeric

-   0.25 tsp cumin powder

-   1 tsp ghee or butter

### Method

1\. Mix lentils, water, turmeric, cumin and salt in a microwave-safe
bowl.

2\. Cover loosely and microwave 1--2 minutes; stir and heat in 30-second
bursts until steaming hot.

3\. Warm rice until steaming hot, stir ghee/butter into dal and serve
together.

## Microwave masala potato chaat

### Ingredients

-   2 tbsp yogurt

-   1 tsp green chutney

-   1 tsp tamarind chutney or ketchup

-   0.25 tsp chaat masala

-   1 tsp lemon juice

### Method

1\. Wash and pierce potato several times with a fork; microwave on a
safe plate 4--6 minutes, turning halfway, until soft. Cool slightly.

3\. Top with yogurt and chutneys if available; mix gently.
